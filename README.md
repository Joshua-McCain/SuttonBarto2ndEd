# SuttonBarto2ndEd
My answers to some of the exercises presented in the chapters of Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction 2nd Edition by Richard Sutton and Andrew Barto

**DISCLAIMER**
This is mostly a personal project that I want to document through Github. As such, I may not answer every question from the book, nor will every answer be perfectly correct.

## Learning Concerning AI
Importantly, I am committed to not using AI until I form a comprehensible answer for the questions I am working on. In the cases I resort to AI, it is because no online search was proving beneficial, and even in this case I only ask AI for hints on what I could be doing better, similar to how a teacher would help a student, since it can be difficult to comprehend the subject when there is nobody to consult.

In summary, this repo is to document how *I* think about problems; My thoughts and only my thoughts are written down on paper in notes and exercises in an effort to show how I approach and solve problems, step by step.

For code, I am coding everything from scratch from the algorithms presented in the book. Only when I encounter bugs or efficiency issues I fail to solve for hours do I use AI for hints on what is going wrong.

## Project Layout

For each chapter I have covered, I will upload my handwritten notes on the topics presented in the chapter. In addition, for the exercises I have time for and deem an interesting challenge to improve my understanding of the material, I will upload handwritten answers or notes pretaining to the problem. I hope it may serve as proof of my reading and document my journey to understanding the beginnings of reinforcement learning.
